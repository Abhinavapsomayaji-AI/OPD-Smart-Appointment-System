# Smart Appointment System for OPD and Medical sector #

## Inspiration 

I got this idea because my father is a doctor and sometimes his OP appointments are overlapping with his surgeries, his free time, etc. Then later I also realized that most of the doctors and patients face this problem of not proper communication or coordination between the doctor and the patient. So, I have decided to make a system where there will be a proper coordinated communication between a doctor and the patient.
